01. Overview

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy describes the general ways information may be collected, used and handled when you use Atelier Journal.

Atelier Journal is an editorial and lifestyle website covering food, fashion, travel, nature, decor and quotes.

By using the website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

02. Information We Collect

We aim to keep information collection limited to what is reasonably necessary for operating and improving the website.

Information You Provide

If you voluntarily contact us or submit information through a feature made available on the website, we may receive information that you choose to provide.

This may include information such as your name, email address or the contents of a message, depending on the feature being used.

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information through public forms or communication channels unless specifically requested and appropriate.

03. Automatically Collected Information

When you visit a website, certain technical information may be automatically processed by the website, hosting provider, analytics services or other technical systems.

Depending on the services used by the website, this information may include:

  • IP address or approximate location information
  • Browser and device information
  • Operating system information
  • Pages visited and referring pages
  • Approximate date and time of visits
  • Technical information used to maintain website security and performance

The exact information collected can depend on the technical services and settings in use at the time of your visit.

04. Cookies & Similar Technologies

Atelier Journal or third-party services used on the website may use cookies or similar technologies.

Cookies are small data files that can help websites remember information, understand usage patterns, maintain functionality or support certain services.

You can generally manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some websites or services.

05. Analytics

We may use analytics tools to understand general website traffic and visitor interactions.

Analytics information can help us understand which pages are useful, identify technical issues and improve the overall website experience.

Analytics providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

06. Third-Party Services

Some features of Atelier Journal may rely on third-party services.

These services may independently collect or process information according to their own privacy policies.

Third-party services may include analytics providers, hosting infrastructure, advertising technology, content services, referral platforms or other external providers.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of third-party services before providing information to them.

07. Third-Party Links & Offers

Atelier Journal may contain links to external websites, products, services, promotions or other third-party destinations.

Some external links may be referral or affiliate links. This means that the website operator may receive compensation if a visitor takes a qualifying action through certain links, where applicable.

Clicking an external link may take you to a website that has its own privacy policy, cookies, tracking technologies and terms.

We do not control the privacy practices of external websites and recommend reviewing their policies before submitting personal information.

08. How Information May Be Used

Information collected through the website may be used for legitimate operational purposes, including:

  • Operating and maintaining the website
  • Improving website content and usability
  • Understanding general visitor activity
  • Responding to communications
  • Detecting security issues or abuse
  • Supporting website analytics
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations

We do not use information for purposes that are materially incompatible with this Privacy Policy without appropriate notice where required.

09. Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information simply because you visit Atelier Journal.

Information may be processed or shared with service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the website, provide technical services, analyze website activity, maintain security or comply with legal requirements.

Third-party websites that you choose to visit through external links operate independently from Atelier Journal.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures intended to protect information handled through the website.

However, no internet transmission, electronic storage system or website can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should use reasonable care when deciding what personal information to provide online.

11. Children's Privacy

Atelier Journal is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the website, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed where appropriate.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, technology, services, legal requirements or privacy practices.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page together with a revised "Last updated" date.

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Atelier Journal, you may contact us using the contact information made available on the website.
